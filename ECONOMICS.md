# Unit Economics

## What Is A Converted Lead Worth To Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits at approximately 20–40% below
retail price. A startup spending $500/month on AI tools represents
$6,000/year in spend. If Credex captures even 50% of that spend
through discounted credits, the annual contract value (ACV) is
approximately $3,000. At a 30% gross margin on credits, that is
$900 gross profit per customer per year.

Conservative estimate: $500–$1,500 lifetime value per converted customer
Aggressive estimate: $3,000–$8,000 for larger teams with higher spend

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) By Channel

| Channel | CAC Estimate | Reasoning |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Hacker News (Show HN) | ~$0 | Time cost only — no paid spend |
| Twitter cold DMs | ~$2–5 | Time cost at $20/hr, 10 DMs/hr, 2% conversion |
| Reddit posts | ~$0 | Time cost only |
| Indie Hackers | ~$0 | Time cost only |
| Paid social (if used) | ~$40–80 | B2B SaaS typical CAC on LinkedIn |

At $0 paid CAC and $900 gross profit per customer, even 10 conversions
per month is $9,000 gross profit monthly.

## What Would Have To Be True For $1M ARR In 18 Months

$1M ARR = ~$83,000/month revenue
At $3,000 ACV per customer = 28 new customers per month
At 2% audit-to-customer conversion = 1,400 audits per month needed
At 30% audit completion rate = 4,700 visitors per month needed

This is very achievable. A single viral HN post drives 1,000–2,000
visitors. Four successful distribution events per month gets there.

The harder constraint is Credex's sales capacity — closing 28 deals
per month requires at least 2 dedicated sales people.

Levers that accelerate this:
1. Embed AuditAI in Cursor/Anthropic partner onboarding flows
2. Add referral mechanic — share your audit, both parties get discount
3. Add benchmark mode — "your spend per developer vs industry average"
   creates urgency and virality simultaneously

## Rough P&L At Scale

Revenue per customer: $3,000 ACV
COGS (credit sourcing): ~70% = $2,100
Gross profit: $900 (30% margin)
CAC (blended): $50
Payback period: 0.7 months
LTV/CAC ratio: 18x

This is an exceptionally healthy unit economics profile for a B2B
SaaS tool because CAC is near zero and the product sells itself
through demonstrated savings.
