you book a Credex consultation for significant savings — but only
if you want to.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price is pulled directly from official vendor pricing pages
and verified weekly. Sources are documented in our public repo.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: Your email and audit results are stored securely in our database
if you choose to share them. Your data is never sold. The public
shareable URL strips your email and company name.

**Q: What tools do you support?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Windsurf,
Anthropic API, and OpenAI API. More tools added weekly.

**Q: What if I'm already spending optimally?**
A: We'll tell you honestly. "You're spending well" is a valid
audit result. We don't manufacture fake savings.
