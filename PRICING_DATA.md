# 📊 Pricing Research – AI Developer Tools

_Last Updated: May 2025_

---

## 🧠 Objective

This document analyzes pricing strategies of popular AI-powered developer tools to understand:
- Market pricing trends
- Value positioning
- Cost structures for individuals and teams

---

## 💻 Cursor (AI Code Editor)

- Hobby: $0/month  
- Pro: $20/user/month  
- Business: $40/user/month  

Source: https://cursor.com/pricing  

**Insights:**
- Freemium model attracts new users
- $20/month aligns with industry standard
- Business tier targets teams with collaboration features

---

## 🤖 Claude (Anthropic)

- Free: $0  
- Pro: $20/user/month  
- Max: $100/user/month  
- Team: $30/user/month  

Source: https://claude.ai/pricing  

**Insights:**
- Wide pricing range (casual → power users)
- High-end plan for heavy usage
- Strong competitor in AI assistant space

---

## 🧑‍💻 GitHub Copilot

- Individual: $10/month  
- Business: $19/user/month  
- Enterprise: $39/user/month  

Source: https://github.com/features/copilot  

**Insights:**
- Lower entry price than competitors
- Strong integration with IDEs
- Widely adopted by developers

---

## 💬 ChatGPT

- Free: $0  
- Plus: $20/user/month  
- Team: $30/user/month (min 2 users)  
- Enterprise: Custom pricing  

Source: https://openai.com/pricing  

**Insights:**
- $20/month is a key benchmark in the market
- Broad use cases beyond coding
- Enterprise focus on scalability and security

---

## 🌐 Gemini (Google)

- Free: $0  
- Advanced (Google One AI Premium): ~$20/month  

Source: https://gemini.google.com  

**Insights:**
- Bundled with Google ecosystem
- Competes directly with ChatGPT Plus
- Adds value through integration with Google services

---

## 🌊 Windsurf (Codeium)

- Pro: ~$10/month  
- Teams: Custom pricing  

Source: https://windsurf.ai

- Aggressive free tier strategy

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

- $20/month is the industry standard pricing tier
- $10/month represents budget-friendly alternatives
- Freemium models dominate user acquisition
- APIs follow usage-based pricing models
- Enterprise plans emphasize:
  - Security
  - Compliance
  - Scalability

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

The AI developer tools market shows clear pricing patterns:

- $10 → Budget tools (Copilot, Windsurf)
- $20 → Standard premium tier (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor)
- $30+ → Team and enterprise solutions
- Usage-based → APIs for scalable products

For new products (e.g., AuditAI), an effective pricing strategy should include:
- A free entry-level tier
- A $10–$20 core plan
- Scalable pricing for teams and businesses
